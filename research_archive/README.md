# Research archive and inclusion policy

Prepared 2026-10-03. This archive preserves supporting work needed to revise or audit the website. The reading entry is `website_handoff/`, not this archive.

`marketing/` contains the original twelve-month study, with its own later observation window and unvalidated strategy interpretations. `withdrawn_customer_instrument/` retains the prepared tool explicitly withdrawn by the user; it contains no conducted interviews. `customer_evidence/` contains de-identified public self-report coding, not a representative customer dataset. `audit/` retains drafting and style-check history. `project_state/` captures the BA project context at handoff.

Source PDFs remain local; their public official URLs and SHA-256 hashes are provided in the manifest. This avoids making a second public distribution of full third-party publications. Repository evidence, source links and provenance suffice for the website handoff; not every local duplicate belongs in the publication path.

Existing repository price/history/finance assets and R1 exports are retained in place. The three pre-existing untracked files were inspected and belong to this project: one style comparison and two R1 report drafts. Their copies are archived under `earlier_drafts/`, explicitly not current canonical reports; their original paths stay untouched and untracked. Local copies and private account handles are not deleted. No passwords, credentials or private customer data are included.
