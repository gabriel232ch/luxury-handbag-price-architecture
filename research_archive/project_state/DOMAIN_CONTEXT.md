# Domain context — Luxury slowdown gap assessment

Updated 2026-10-03. Minimum domain calibration, not a reusable Domain Pack.

- Boundary: personal luxury goods; Chanel consolidated House, Fashion, and handbags are distinct analytical levels. Handbag list-price observations do not measure realized prices, volumes or consolidated mix.
- Reporting: compare constant-currency/comparable growth direction with explicit company definitions. LVMH Fashion & Leather Goods is multi-brand; no LV/Dior standalone financial inference. Separate recurring and reported operating profit where relevant.
- Demand: customer nationality and purchase destination differ; regional revenue is not a China-customer spending series. Public industry customer estimates cannot establish Chanel's client concentration.
- Timing: show, campaign launch, product delivery and sales period differ; recent creative activity cannot explain earlier business outcomes without time-consistent evidence.
- Economics: distinguish demand resilience from cash/financial capacity to maintain investment. Capex affects cash and subsequent depreciation, not immediate operating expense in full.
- Source lineage: LS-S02/04/05/06 in SOURCE_MAP; LS-E01/03/04/05/06 in EVIDENCE_LEDGER. Category weights, client cohorts and price-volume-mix contributions remain unresolved.

## Localization calibration — 2026-10-03

- Chanel AP includes Japan; Hermès AP excludes Japan and reports it separately. Do not treat the labels as identical comparison populations.
- Exact reported-currency geography bridges locate dollar movement; they retain FX/scope/mix. Constant-currency growth is a separate metric, not an interchangeable bridge input.
- Industry estimates can be revised across annual releases. Separate publication vintage, historical period and forecast/estimate status before comparing growth.
- Category mix is now a live project hypothesis, not established Chanel demand evidence; missing numeric Fashion/Beauty/handbag splits remain critical for attribution.

## Full-accounts calibration — 2026-10-03

- FY2024/FY2025 consolidated accounts now support functional cost and retail/wholesale splits. Earlier release-only disclosure gaps are not claims of universal non-disclosure.
- Staff costs are by nature; SG&A/cost of sales/advertising/distribution are by function. Overlapping views cannot be blindly added.
- Chanel adjusted EBITDA excludes exceptional items and selected DA; ROU depreciation is excluded from DA addback.
- Profit recovery must name its level: FY2025 operating improves while tax-after declines; finance FX cannot explain operating-profit changes.
- Gross margin shift remains a mix/price/volume/production/FX question; consolidated cost identity cannot select among these.

## Channel / calendar calibration — 2026-10-03

- Retail dollar contribution is partly initial scale; channel share stable at~77% inFY2024/25 despite larger Retail contribution. Not a newly proven DTC pivot.
- Wholesale confirmation not terminal sell-through. Retail includes multiple business categories; no automatic handbags/Beauty partition.
- Collection year may differ from show year and arrival year. BlazySS2026 shownOct2025, arrivesMar2026. Product-sales channel excluded from earlier year; brand halo remains separate.
- Annual operational highlights are management interpretations, not product sales bridges. Multiple brand/press articles can share a campaign-source cluster.

## Customer / external-category calibration — 2026-10-03

- Public first purchases do not establish brand incrementality: next-best Chanel product and timing/budget counterfactual matter. Product usage substitution differs from purchase cannibalization.
- Public user reports require actor/date/outcome distinctions; thread date not necessarily comment/purchase date, first interest not first purchase, hoped-for return not completed return.
- Third-party beauty rankings can provide scale despite no audited category bridge. Match graph period, USD conversion, source vintage and category definition before comparing estimates or calculating non-Beauty remainder.
- Promotion launch is not universally first retail availability; early ownership leads need SKU/market confirmation and do not become authoritative first-sale dates.
